Why Vizory

Simple on purpose. Priced that way too.

One flat price, no upgrade pressure. Sortly's free plan is genuinely useful to start, but it's capped at 100 items and doesn't include export or offline access. The moment your shop grows past that, you're looking at $49/month minimum. Vizory is one price, no tiers, no feature-gating as you grow.

Built around how solo sellers actually work. Sortly's structure — folders, subfolders, multi-location tracking, team permissions — is designed for businesses managing physical assets across sites and staff. Vizory keeps it to what a one-person operation actually uses: SKUs, barcode scanning, stock levels, and low-stock alerts.

No item ceiling to plan around. Whether you're tracking 30 SKUs or 300, Vizory doesn't ask you to upgrade plans as your catalog grows.
